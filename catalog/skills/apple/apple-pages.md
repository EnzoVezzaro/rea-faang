## What it is

Pages is Apple’s word processor and page-layout app for macOS and iOS, with Apple-native and Word-compatible documents.

## Core capabilities

- Word processing and page layout
- Word/docx import and export
- Templates, shapes, media placement

## Existing open source

| Project | License | What it covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [LibreOffice Writer](https://www.libreoffice.org/) | MPL-2.0 | Full word processor with strong format support. | The main open replacement. |
| [OnlyOffice Docs](https://www.onlyoffice.com/) | AGPL-3.0 | Collaborative word processing. | Strong MS-format fidelity. |
| [Calligra Words](https://calligra.org/) | GPL-2.0 | KDE word processor with layout features. | Alternative in the KDE stack. |

## Gap

Pages’ own .pages format is undocumented; round-tripping Apple documents is lossy.

## Verdict

Available. Mature open word processors cover the function.
