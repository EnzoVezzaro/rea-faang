## What it is

Keynote is Apple’s presentation app, with cinematic transitions, Magic Move and Apple-native document formats.

## Core capabilities

- Slide authoring and transitions
- Presenting and presenter mode
- PowerPoint/pptx import and export

## Existing open source

| Project | License | What it covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [LibreOffice Impress](https://www.libreoffice.org/) | MPL-2.0 | Full slide authoring and presenting, pptx-compatible. | The main desktop replacement. |
| [reveal.js](https://revealjs.com/) | MIT | HTML/JS presentation framework. | Code-first slides. |
| [Marp / Slidev](https://marp.app/) | MIT | Markdown-to-slides toolchains. | Developer-oriented authoring. |
| [Calligra Stage](https://calligra.org/) | GPL-2.0 | KDE presentation app. | Alternative in the KDE stack. |

## Gap

Keynote’s proprietary format and signature transitions have no exact open match.

## Verdict

Available. Both desktop and web/markdown open presentation tools exist.
