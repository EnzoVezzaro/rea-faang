## What it is

Final Cut Pro is Apple’s professional non-linear video editor, with a magnetic timeline, proxy workflows and Apple Silicon acceleration.

## Core capabilities

- Non-linear multi-track editing
- Effects, colour, titles, audio
- Proxy and optimised media workflows
- Hardware-accelerated encoding

## Existing open source

| Project | License | What it covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org/) | GPL-3.0 | Mature professional-grade NLE with proxies. | Closest open equivalent. |
| [Shotcut](https://shotcut.org/) | GPL-3.0 | Cross-platform NLE, wide format support. | Broad codec support. |
| [OpenShot](https://www.openshot.org/) | GPL-3.0 | Beginner-friendly editor. | Simpler feature set. |
| [Olive / Blender VSE](https://www.olivevideoeditor.org/) | GPL-3.0 / GPL-2.0 | Node-based and integrated editing options. | Alternative workflows. |

## Gap

Deep Apple Silicon acceleration and some proprietary codec/plug-in pipelines are not matched.

## Verdict

Available. Several mature open NLEs cover professional editing.
