## What it is

TensorFlow is Google’s open-source machine-learning framework, released under Apache-2.0 and used for training and serving models across platforms.

## Core capabilities

- Tensor computation with GPU/TPU acceleration
- Keras high-level training API
- Serving, mobile (Lite) and browser (TF.js) runtimes

## Existing open source

| Project | License | What it covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [TensorFlow itself](https://github.com/tensorflow/tensorflow) | Apache-2.0 | TensorFlow is already free and open source. | The product is the open-source project. |
| [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/) | BSD-3-Clause | Open framework and the dominant research ecosystem. | Independent open alternative. |
| [JAX](https://github.com/jax-ml/jax) | Apache-2.0 | Open numerical/ML framework from Google. | Functional, XLA-based. |

## Gap

None in licensing — the framework is already open source.

## Verdict

Available (already open source). Apache-2.0, with open alternatives in PyTorch and JAX.
