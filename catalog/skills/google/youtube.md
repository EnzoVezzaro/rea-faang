## What it is

YouTube is a video hosting and streaming platform: upload, transcode, deliver across a global CDN, advertise, and recommend.

## Core capabilities

- Video hosting, transcoding and adaptive streaming
- Channels, subscriptions, playlists, comments
- Recommendations and search
- Monetisation and advertising

## Existing open source

| Project | License | What it covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [PeerTube](https://joinpeertube.org/) | AGPL-3.0 | Federated video hosting and streaming platform. | The real platform-level alternative. |
| [Invidious](https://invidious.io/) | AGPL-3.0 | Private alternative front end to YouTube. | Front end only; depends on YouTube. |
| [Piped](https://piped.video/) | AGPL-3.0 | Alternative YouTube front end. | Front end only. |
| [FreeTube / NewPipe](https://freetubeapp.io/) | AGPL-3.0 / GPL-3.0 | Desktop and Android YouTube clients. | Clients, not platforms. |

## Gap

The software is replaceable; the licensed content library and creator monetisation are not.

## Verdict

Available. PeerTube is a fully open hosting platform; the gap is content and network effects rather than code.
