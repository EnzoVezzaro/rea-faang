Research batch plan

Snapshot: 2026-11-02. This plan turns the pending research into ordered, reviewable batches. It does not mark any dossier as researched; promotion happens only when a dossier's evidence is filled and the validators pass.

There is no catalog inventory gap. The remaining work is depth: every dossier is research_status: pending, and the counts above describe candidate research, not verified product parity.

Batch rules

  • One batch is one review unit; do not mix companies in a promotion handoff unless the batch says so.
  • Sources: official product pages, official documentation, published pricing, published protocols, and unauthenticated help-centre pages. No private surfaces, no decompilation, no access-control bypass.
  • Each material claim gets a primary source in evidence[]. Unknowns are recorded as unknowns — never filled with inference.
  • An alternative is described by what it verifiably covers; no parity claims.
  • Definition of done for a dossier: sections 1–15 filled, research_status: researched, last_researched_at set, research_confidence justified, evidence[] populated, and npx rae-faang research validate clean.

Batch 6 — Products with a mature candidate listed (40)

All remaining catalogued products with available coverage. Research is still pending and must not be inferred from the existence of an alternative. Group by company and process after Batches 1–5, because a candidate already exists and the marginal value of a new dossier is lower.

Blockers and limits (real)

  1. No private or binary inspection. Platform-level behaviour that is only observable in binaries or behind authentication stays an explicit unknown. No Hopper/Ghidra/IDA analysis is installed or authorized here.
  2. Public-source rate limits. REA captures and documentation reads must respect robots.txt and site terms; a failed capture is recorded, not retried abusively.
  3. Evidence review is human. A dossier is promoted only after a reviewer confirms each material claim traces to a cited source; automation cannot grant confidence.
  4. No parity claims. Alternative coverage labels describe research, not build feasibility or product parity.

Suggested next action
