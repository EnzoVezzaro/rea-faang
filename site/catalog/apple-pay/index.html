At a glance

Apple Pay is Apple's contactless payment and wallet service.

Catalog categories: payments.

Catalogued capability areas: - NFC contactless payments - In-app and web checkout - Cards stored in Wallet

Coverage and gaps

Alternative coverage

No verified alternative. The payment rails are closed; no open-source equivalent exists.

The gap

Payments sit on proprietary bank and card-network rails; no open-source project can replicate acceptance or tokenization.
