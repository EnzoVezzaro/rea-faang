The engineering premise

One product. One skill. A clearer place to start.

Each product's Markdown file is the build skill for that product. Give it to your coding agent in the project where you want to build. It provides product context, research sources, open-source projects to evaluate, and directions for implementing and verifying the product.

Your agent does the building in your project. The skill helps it reuse what fits, define a clear scope from the evidence, then build and verify an independent version of the product.

  • Product context — behavior and sources for your agent to inspect
  • What to reuse — candidate open-source foundations to evaluate
  • What to build — product-specific direction for your agent
  • How to verify — scoped tasks and checks for the result

Common questions

How do I use a product skill?

How do I build a product with my agent?

Choose a product and download its Markdown skill, or generate the kit with the RAE CLI. Give the skill to your coding agent in your project. Its instructions cover product research, reuse decisions, building and verification.

What does the catalog add beyond candidate projects?

Each entry also includes product context and a skill with build directions. Use those to review the research, decide what to reuse and scope the work for your project. Coverage labels describe the catalog's research, not the feasibility of your implementation.

What sources can the research use?

The research uses public product information, official documentation, published protocols and observations of unauthenticated web pages. It does not use proprietary source, decompile software, copy protected assets or bypass access controls.

What should I check before building?

Review the cited sources, candidate project licenses and any third-party rights that apply to your implementation. Product names are functional references; no affiliation or endorsement is implied. Rights holders can raise an issue on the repository.

Do I need to be an expert to use a product skill?

No. Choose a product, give its skill file to your coding agent, and ask it to follow the research, reuse and verification instructions in your project. The CLI and docs/getting-started.md show how to get the skill.

What does “no verified alternative” mean?

The catalog has not verified a suitable open-source project for the same user needs. It does not mean you cannot build the product. Give its Markdown skill to your coding agent; the agent can assess current projects and build in your project.

How can I check a product entry?

Open its research dossier and follow the cited sources. Where available, the product page also links to a REA report with observations from a public web page. npm run validate checks record structure and generated files; it does not confirm that every research claim is correct or current.
