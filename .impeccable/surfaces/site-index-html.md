## Surface

Generated static website: landing page, searchable catalog, product dossiers, and docs. Mode: Persuade at the landing and Read/Operate on its catalog, dossiers, and documentation.

## Audience and job

Developers choose a product they want their coding agent to build in their own project. Success is finding a product skill quickly and downloading or generating it. Evidence and coverage should answer reuse questions without claiming build status.

## Chosen direction

Catalog Glyph Field — the real product inventory becomes the landing's visual signature rather than a decorative terminal. This is the rolled lead from surface concept seed `3121ab48`, chosen after the user said “continue”; existing RAE identity remains pinned. The interface uses actual product names and catalog links, with the interactive Product Build Pass directly below the field. The full page carries that typographic inventory language into catalog exploration and evidence, replacing repeated feature cards with readable names, rows, and editorial proof.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make RAE’s real product inventory the hero image; refuse the generic SaaS feature-card sequence and simulated agent console.
OWN-WORLD: Keep RAE near-black, warm off-white, signal lime, Space Grotesk / Inter / IBM Plex Mono, supplied lockup, crisp editorial typography, and precise touchable controls.
STORY: Visitors see the real product range, select one, understand its reuse research honestly, and download a product-specific skill for their own coding agent.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Global header; left-aligned two-line manifesto and direct catalog action beside a large readable field of linked product names. Put the catalog-derived personalized pass directly beneath; on mobile make these distinct single-column stages with no horizontal overflow.
FORM: Catalog Glyph Field, rolled lead, seed `3121ab48`; live glyph-field interaction is a real product selection that updates the pass, never ornamental text.

This redesign applies to the homepage only. Keep global RAE tokens, logos, navigation, taxonomy, focus treatment, catalogue data, evidence, product links, downloads, accessibility, and reduced-motion support. Other pages retain their established layouts.
